Battery Checker (ESP32)

  • INA219: voltage and current
  • MLX90614: surface temperature
  • VL53L0X: swelling thickness
  • MQ-2 gas sensor: leakage fumes
  • OLED and RGB LED show the risk level

SafeBin Node

  • Sand-lined metal compartment
  • Temperature and smoke sensors with buzzer
  • HX711 load cell weighs each drop
  • Servo lock opens only for a valid QR or RFID token
  • WiFi/MQTT alerts to the dashboard
